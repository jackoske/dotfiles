description: Explain the available supervised workflows and when to use them.
---

Summarize the available workflow entry points and their appropriate use:
`/herdr-orchestrate` for supervised multi-agent work, `/plan` for plan-first
work, `/remember` for approved durable memories, `/safety-review` for a
read-only risk review, `/status` for a concise progress view, `/handoff` for
continuity, `/checkpoint` for an approved durable project-local record,
`/goal` for creating or clarifying a durable goal record, `/resume` for
validating and continuing resumable work, and `/evidence` for appending factual
verification evidence.
Mention that material product, safety, scope, or data decisions use the native
structured question flow; approved work uses a visible todo list and concise
phase updates; reviewers remain read-only and children cannot spawn
grandchildren. Describe behavior, not unavailable live state, and do not edit
files.
