Summarize the available workflow entry points and their appropriate use:
`/herdr-orchestrate` for supervised multi-agent work, `/status` for a concise
progress view, `/handoff` for continuity, and `/checkpoint` for an approved
durable project-local record. Mention that material product, safety, scope, or
data decisions use the native structured question flow; approved work uses a
visible todo list and concise phase updates; reviewers remain read-only and
children cannot spawn grandchildren. Describe behavior, not unavailable live
state, and do not edit files.
